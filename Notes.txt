-Main lesson for Nepal: the technology can work, but allowing each court to build separate AI solutions eventually creates duplication and governance problems. Nepal should define a common national architecture, metadata standard, model governance framework, and shared evaluation process from the beginning.
-AI cannot replace judges in making judicial decisions; AI results should remain supplementary references.
-Main lesson for Nepal: AI works much better when courts already have structured electronic files, interoperable systems, online services, and reliable digital data. This strongly supports building Nepal’s digital legal foundation before advanced AI.
-with our Authority-Aware RAG, Nepal could actually take a more cautious technical path than some earlier international projects: verified legal data first, then retrieval and authority checking, then generation, citation verification, and finally human review.
