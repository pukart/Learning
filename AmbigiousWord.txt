1. Judicial discretion (न्यायाधीशको विवेकाधिकार)
- Judicial discretion means the freedom given to a judge to choose the most appropriate decision within the limits of law, especially when the law does not prescribe one exact outcome.

2. LEGAL-BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers)
- LEGAL-BERT is further trained on legal materials such as legislation, court decisions, contracts, and other legal documents, so it understands legal terminology and patterns better than a general-purpose BERT model.

	In very simple terms:
	BERT = a model that reads text deeply to understand what it means.
	ChatGPT-style LLM = a model that understands text and also generates new text.

3. Brazil shows value of narrow, high-volume case classification mean AI can provide strong practical value when it is used for a small, well-defined task that needs to be repeated across thousands of cases.

4. Tribunals (न्यायाधिकरण)
- Tribunals are specialized bodies that decide disputes in a particular area of law. They usually deal with a specific subject, such as tax, labor, immigration, administrative decisions, or competition matters. For example, a tax tribunal hears tax disputes, while a labor tribunal may hear disputes between employees and employers.

5. Structured system
- information arranged in an organized format so it can be easily searched, connected, analyzed, and reused.

6. Nuance (अर्थको सूक्ष्म पक्ष)
- subtle detail that affects how something should be understood.

7. Grounded LLM analysis
- means analysis produced by a large language model that is based on specific, verifiable source material, rather than only on the model’s general learned knowledge.
- Grounded LLM analysis = AI analysis tied to authoritative and traceable source documents.

8. Unicode normalization
- making text internally consistent so computers can reliably search, compare, process, and index it.

9. BM25 → good for exact legal terms, section numbers, names, citations
   Vector search → good for similar meaning even when wording is different
- Using both together is called hybrid retrieval, and it is often very effective for legal RAG systems.
