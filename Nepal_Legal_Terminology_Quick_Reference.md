# Nepal Legal Terminology – Quick Reference

## For Judicial Research, Court Documents, and Judicial AI

This reference groups common Nepali legal terms by function. English equivalents are practical translations; exact meaning can depend on the statute, court document, and context.

\---

## 1\. Sources and Forms of Law

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|संविधान|Constitution|The supreme law of Nepal. Other laws must be consistent with it.|
|ऐन|Act / Statute|A law enacted by the legislature.|
|संहिता|Code|A systematically organized body of law covering a broad legal subject.|
|नियमावली|Rules / Regulation|Detailed subordinate rules made under authority granted by an Act.|
|विनियमावली|Regulations / By-laws|Detailed regulatory provisions made by an authorized body under law.|
|निर्देशिका|Directive / Guidelines|Operational directions issued under legal or administrative authority.|
|कार्यविधि|Procedure / Procedural Rules|Prescribed process for carrying out judicial or administrative functions.|
|आदेश|Order|A binding direction or ruling issued by a court or competent authority.|
|अध्यादेश|Ordinance|A temporary law issued under constitutional authority when the legislature is not in session, subject to constitutional conditions.|
|विधेयक|Bill|A proposed law submitted to the legislature for enactment.|
|प्रचलित कानून|Prevailing / Applicable Law|Law currently in force and applicable to a matter.|

## 2\. Structure of Constitution, Acts, and Codes

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|भाग|Part|A major division of a Constitution, Act, or Code.|
|परिच्छेद|Chapter|A group of related legal provisions.|
|धारा|Article|A main numbered provision of the Constitution.|
|उपधारा|Clause / Paragraph under an Article|A subdivision of a constitutional Article.|
|दफा|Section|A main numbered provision of an Act or Code.|
|उपदफा|Subsection|A subdivision of a Section.|
|खण्ड|Clause|A smaller subdivision within an Article, Section, or Subsection.|
|उपखण्ड|Sub-clause|A subdivision of a Clause.|
|प्रावधान / व्यवस्था|Provision|A specific legal rule, right, duty, power, condition, or requirement.|
|प्रतिबन्धात्मक वाक्यांश / परन्तु व्यवस्था|Proviso|A qualification, exception, condition, or limitation attached to a main provision.|
|स्पष्टीकरण|Explanation|Text clarifying the meaning, scope, or application of a provision.|
|अनुसूची|Schedule|Forms, tables, classifications, lists, or supporting material attached to and forming part of a law.|
|प्रस्तावना|Preamble|Introductory statement explaining the purpose, object, or background of a law.|
|परिभाषा|Definition|A provision assigning a specific legal meaning to a word or expression.|

## 3\. Court, Case, and Proceeding Terms

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|मुद्दा|Case / Legal Proceeding|A dispute, claim, prosecution, petition, or proceeding before a court.|
|मुद्दा नम्बर|Case Number|Official identifier assigned to a case.|
|मिसिल|Case File / Court Record|The official collection of pleadings, evidence, orders, and other records of a case.|
|निवेदन|Petition / Application|A formal written request submitted to a court or authority.|
|रिट निवेदन|Writ Petition|A petition seeking an extraordinary constitutional or judicial remedy.|
|फिरादपत्र|Plaint / Statement of Claim|Formal document by which a civil claim is instituted.|
|प्रतिउत्तरपत्र|Written Statement / Defence|Formal response filed by the defendant or respondent.|
|अभियोगपत्र|Charge Sheet / Indictment|Formal accusation setting out criminal charges, depending on procedural context.|
|पेशी|Hearing / Cause Listing|A scheduled court appearance or listing of a matter for hearing.|
|इजलास|Bench|The judge or panel of judges hearing a matter; also used for the sitting of the court.|
|बहस|Oral Argument / Pleading|Oral submissions made before the court.|
|बहस नोट|Written Submission / Written Argument|Written legal arguments submitted to the court.|

## 4\. Parties and Participants

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|वादी|Plaintiff / Claimant|Party who initiates a civil claim.|
|प्रतिवादी|Defendant|Party against whom a civil or criminal proceeding is brought; context matters.|
|निवेदक|Petitioner / Applicant|Person or entity filing a petition or application.|
|प्रत्यर्थी / विपक्षी|Respondent / Opposing Party|Party against whom a petition, appeal, or application is directed.|
|अभियुक्त|Accused|Person charged with a criminal offence.|
|जाहेरवाला|Complainant / Informant|Person who reports or initiates a criminal complaint, depending on the case.|
|साक्षी|Witness|Person who gives testimony or evidence.|
|कानून व्यवसायी|Legal Practitioner / Advocate|Lawyer representing or advising a party.|

## 5\. Judgment and Judicial Reasoning

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|फैसला|Judgment / Decision|Court's formal determination of a case.|
|आदेश|Court Order|Judicial direction or ruling that may be interim or final.|
|ठहर|Holding / Determination|What the court determines on a particular issue or charge.|
|निर्णयाधार / फैसलाको आधार|Reasoning / Grounds of Decision|Legal and factual reasons supporting the court's conclusion.|
|निर्णयको निष्कर्ष|Conclusion / Disposition|Final outcome reached by the court.|
|नजिर|Precedent|Earlier judicial decision treated as legal authority in a later case.|
|प्रतिपादित सिद्धान्त|Judicially Established Legal Principle|Legal principle formulated, clarified, or applied by a court.|
|न्यायिक व्याख्या|Judicial Interpretation|Meaning assigned to constitutional or statutory provisions by a court.|
|अनुपातिक निर्णयाधार (Ratio Decidendi)|Ratio Decidendi|Legal principle necessary to the court's decision and potentially authoritative as precedent.|
|प्रसंगवश अभिव्यक्ति (Obiter Dictum)|Obiter Dictum|Judicial observation not necessary to decide the case; persuasive rather than the core holding.|

## 6\. Facts, Evidence, Claims, and Arguments

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|तथ्य|Fact|A circumstance or event relevant to the dispute.|
|तथ्यगत प्रश्न|Question of Fact|Question concerning what actually happened.|
|कानूनी प्रश्न|Question of Law|Question concerning interpretation or application of law.|
|प्रमाण|Evidence / Proof|Material relied on to prove or disprove a fact.|
|साक्ष्य|Evidence / Testimony|Evidence presented in a legal proceeding; may include oral, documentary, or other legally admissible material.|
|दस्तावेजी प्रमाण|Documentary Evidence|Evidence contained in documents or records.|
|मौखिक साक्ष्य|Oral Evidence / Testimony|Evidence given orally by a witness.|
|दाबी|Claim|Legal assertion, demand, or relief sought by a party.|
|जिकिर|Contention / Argument|Legal or factual position advanced by a party.|
|मागदाबी|Relief Sought / Prayer|Specific remedy or order requested from the court.|
|प्रमाणको भार|Burden of Proof|Legal responsibility to prove a fact or allegation.|

## 7\. Jurisdiction, Authority, and Interpretation

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|अधिकारक्षेत्र|Jurisdiction|Legal authority of a court or body to hear and decide a matter.|
|क्षेत्राधिकार|Territorial / Subject-matter Jurisdiction|Scope of authority based on territory, subject, or legal competence; usage depends on context.|
|कानूनी आधार|Legal Basis / Legal Authority|Constitutional, statutory, regulatory, or precedential authority supporting a claim or decision.|
|कानूनको व्याख्या|Statutory / Legal Interpretation|Process of determining the meaning and application of legal provisions.|
|संवैधानिक व्याख्या|Constitutional Interpretation|Judicial interpretation of constitutional provisions and principles.|
|बाध्यकारी नजिर|Binding Precedent|Precedent that a lower or equivalent court is legally required to follow where applicable.|
|दृष्टान्त / persuasive authority|Persuasive Authority|Decision or legal source that may guide but is not necessarily binding.|

\---

## 8\. Appeals, Review, and Remedies

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|पुनरावेदन|Appeal|Request to a higher court to review a lower court's decision.|
|पुनरावलोकन|Review|Reconsideration of a judgment under legally prescribed conditions.|
|दोहोर्‍याई पाऊँ|Petition for Review / Leave to Reconsider|A procedural request for reconsideration in circumstances allowed by law; exact English label depends on context.|
|अन्तरिम आदेश|Interim Order|Temporary order issued before final disposal of the case.|
|निषेधाज्ञा|Injunction|Court order restraining or requiring certain conduct, depending on legal context.|
|उपचार|Remedy|Legal means by which a right is enforced or a wrong is addressed.|
|क्षतिपूर्ति|Compensation / Damages|Monetary or other compensation awarded for legally recognized loss or injury.|
|बदर|Quash / Annul / Set Aside|To invalidate a decision, order, or legal act.|
|खारेज|Dismiss / Reject|To terminate or reject a case, petition, application, or claim.|
|खारेजी|Repeal / Revocation / Dismissal|Removal of legal effect; translation must follow context—law may be repealed, an administrative instrument revoked, and a case dismissed.|

## 9\. Constitutional Writs

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|बन्दीप्रत्यक्षीकरण|Habeas Corpus|Remedy against unlawful detention.|
|परमादेश|Mandamus|Order directing a public authority to perform a legal duty.|
|उत्प्रेषण|Certiorari|Order used to quash an unlawful decision or proceeding of a lower body or authority.|
|प्रतिषेध|Prohibition|Order preventing a lower court or authority from acting unlawfully or beyond jurisdiction.|
|अधिकारपृच्छा|Quo Warranto|Inquiry into the legal authority by which a person holds a public office.|

\---

## 10\. Criminal Law Terms

|Nepali Term|English Equivalent|Short Meaning|
|-|-|-|
|कसूर|Offence|Act or omission punishable by law.|
|अपराध|Crime / Offence|Conduct prohibited and punishable under criminal law.|
|अभियोजन|Prosecution|Legal process of pursuing criminal charges.|
|सजाय|Punishment / Sentence|Legal consequence imposed after conviction.|
|कैद|Imprisonment|Sentence requiring confinement.|
|जरिवाना|Fine|Monetary penalty imposed under law.|
|दोषी ठहर|Conviction / Found Guilty|Judicial finding that an accused committed the offence.|
|सफाइ|Acquittal|Judicial determination that the accused is not convicted of the charge.|

## Fast Memory Guide

**Constitution → धारा → Article**  
**Act / Code → दफा → Section**  
**उपदफा → Subsection**  
**खण्ड → Clause**  
**उपखण्ड → Sub-clause**

**Judgment structure:**  
Facts → Claims/Contentions → Legal Issues → Evidence → Applicable Law → Precedent → Reasoning → Holding → Remedy / Final Order

**Writs:**  
Habeas Corpus → बन्दीप्रत्यक्षीकरण  
Mandamus → परमादेश  
Certiorari → उत्प्रेषण  
Prohibition → प्रतिषेध  
Quo Warranto → अधिकारपृच्छा
